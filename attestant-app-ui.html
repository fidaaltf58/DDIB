Off by default. When on, organisations searching for candidates against a credential or XP filter can find you before you've applied anywhere.
This only controls the Operator's search index — it isn't encryption and it isn't access control. Your credentials are independently verifiable on-chain by anyone who already has your public key, regardless of this setting. Turning it off means organisations can't find you first; it doesn't hide what's already published.
